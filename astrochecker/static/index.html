      How to read the result

      Metodo e limiti dell’alfa

        The bundled catalogs provide ICRS coordinates. AstroChecker calculates estimated windows, altitude, azimuth, and Sun locally, without refraction or remote requests. The one-second decision grid is a calculation resolution, not a promise of one-second astronomical accuracy. Weather, Moon, sky quality, and equipment are not evaluated.
